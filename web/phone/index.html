Early warning for crowd crushes, using the phones already in the crowd.

Open in Chrome

Shares motion numbers and your spot in the venue. No name, contacts, audio or photos.

What exactly is shared?

A random session ID, your spot on the venue map (in metres; if GPS is used it is turned into that spot and never stored) and motion numbers ten times a second. Phones running Pulse near you pass each other the same numbers (with a random handle instead of the ID) so the crowd can still be heard when one phone loses its connection. Leave at any time to see everything that was collected.
