    Privacy

    This page only asks for Bluetooth devices whose name starts with PULSE-: the venue’s own boards. It never sees or reports any other Bluetooth device near you. What leaves the phone is the signal strength of those boards (scan mode), or a random id written to a board you chose (connect mode). No names, no addresses, no location history.
