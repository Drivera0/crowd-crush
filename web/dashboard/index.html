      Watch areas

      How areas work
      • An area is one spot you want watched on its own: a barrier, a gate, the stage front.
      • Pulse counts the phones inside and raises an alert when a crowd push travels through it, or when it gets more crowded than you allow.
      • Alert early uses lower thresholds, for spots where trouble starts fast. Standard suits open floor.
      • Each area can light its own zone light, show on the signs and give staff its own message.

          Saved runs

          replay or record

          A saved run is a recording of phones' readings. Playing one feeds it through the same detector as live phones: a rehearsal, and a fallback for the demo if phones can't connect.

          Record a run

          Send a drill

          A drill is a test alert you send on purpose, to check that the briefing, the voice, the signs and the lights all work before doors open.

          The area or zone the drill is about. Its own alert message and zone light are used.

          Level
          Kind of incident

          Sets the wording of the briefing, so staff hear what they would hear in that kind of incident.

          What to test

            What a drill does and does not do
            • It is marked DRILL on every console: the alert card, the timeline and a banner across the top. The spoken briefing starts with “This is a drill”.
            • It never escalates, and the AI leaves it out when asked what happened.
            • Attendees’ phones are not affected: their screens only follow the real detector.
            • Signs and zone lights you include show the level for 8 seconds, then go back to the real state. The boards cannot show the word “drill”, so tell anyone who can see them.
