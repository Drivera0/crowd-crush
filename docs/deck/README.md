# Pulse pitch deck

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser: all 13 slides at 16:9 with speaker notes under each. Print it (Save as PDF, landscape, no margins) for a PDF.

- `slides/*.html`: one file per slide, 1920 × 1080, inline styles only.
- `deck.json`: slide order, sections and the font.
- Slide content and sources: [../DECK.md](../DECK.md). The spoken script: [../PITCH.md](../PITCH.md).

Every number on the slides comes from [../EVAL.md](../EVAL.md), [../LOCATE.md](../LOCATE.md), [../loadtest.md](../loadtest.md) or the table measurement in [../DEMO.md](../DEMO.md), and is simulator or synthetic-phone data unless a slide says otherwise. Slide 2's figure (159, Itaewon, 29 October 2022; 158 died in the alley, and the official toll also counts a survivor who died days later) is from Korea Herald and Inquirer/AFP reporting.
