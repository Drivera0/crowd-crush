Built to run an event

One Go server. 1,000 phones. Nothing dropped.

Console

Setup checklist, watch areas with rules, alert cards with Acknowledge and Resolve, alert drills, escalation.

Resilient

Phones relay for each other over WebRTC. Sign and lights run over USB, no Wi-Fi. Every outside service falls back.

Private

A random ID, venue metres and motion numbers. No names, contacts, audio or photos. Raw GPS never stored.

Load test: 1,000 fake phones at 10 readings a second, about 10,000 messages a second, dashboard steady at 10 Hz (loadtest.md).

Generator ran on the same machine as the server.
