What each sponsor does

Tiger Data

Every motion reading and alert in TimescaleDB hypertables, batched with COPY; a per-zone, per-second continuous aggregate; recorded runs replay from it.

Google Gemini

Writes each alert as one headline and one action, as structured JSON, and reads uploaded floor plans. Never decides what is dangerous; 5 s timeout, then a template.

ElevenLabs

Speaks every briefing aloud for stewards who are not watching the screen, and re-voices alerts nobody acknowledged.

.Tech domain

The short HTTPS address behind the join QR code. Phones need HTTPS for their motion sensors.

Every service can be down or missing its key: Pulse still detects and alerts, with local files, template sentences and the browser's voice.
