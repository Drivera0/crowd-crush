The insight

A crush is a push that travels from person to person.

Dancing: everyone at once

Same motion, same moment. Ignored.

A push: one after another

Same motion, a fraction of a second later, person to person.

Pulse compares each phone with its neighbours. The same motion a fraction of a second later, passed through three people in a row: that's a push. The same motion at the same moment: that's a dance, and it's ignored.
